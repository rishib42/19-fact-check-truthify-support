Truthify

AI fact checker for iPhone. Verify what you read and hear: check text, screenshots, shared posts, live speech and recordings against public web sources.

How it works

Truthify finds the factual claims in what you give it, searches the web for each one and rates it from 1 to 10 for how well independent sources support it, with the sources listed so you can read them yourself.

Plans

  • Free: 3 checks and 3 minutes of live listening to try the app.
  • Pro: 15 checks and 15 minutes of audio a day, including audio and video files.
  • Max: 30 checks and 30 minutes of audio a day, with files up to 30 minutes long.

Pro and Max start with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Daily allowances renew at midnight.

Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone? Open Truthify › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? In the iPhone Settings app, tap your name › Subscriptions › Truthify. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Can I share from Safari or other apps? Yes. Tap Share and choose Truthify to check the text on screen, a selection, a post or a screenshot.

Are the ratings always right? No. Ratings are made with the help of AI and can be wrong; always read the sources shown with each claim.
