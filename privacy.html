Privacy Policy

Truthify · Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

Truthify has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. To check claims, the text or audio you choose to check is sent to Truthify's server and to the AI and web search services it uses. Your history of checks stays on your iPhone.

What is sent to check claims

Only what you choose to check leaves your iPhone, over an encrypted connection:

  • Text you type or paste.
  • Photos and screenshots: the text is read on your iPhone, and only that text is sent, not the image.
  • Pages and posts shared from other apps: the text you shared or the text visible on screen, the page's title and its link.
  • Audio: while you are listening, the microphone recording in short clips; or an audio or video file you choose.

Truthify's server, hosted on Amazon Web Services, sends this content to the AI services it uses to transcribe speech, find the claims in it and rate them. It searches the web for each claim using a short search phrase, through web search services. These providers process the content only to answer that request, under their own data policies.

What the server keeps

  • Audio is processed in memory and temporary files, and is deleted when the check ends.
  • To answer repeated checks quickly, results for typed, photo and shared text are cached for up to 24 hours, and search results for up to 7 days.
  • Your network's IP address is held in memory for up to an hour to limit excessive use of the service.
  • Error logs may include technical details of a failed check, without any account or device identifier.

Nothing is linked to your name, Apple ID or device.

On your iPhone

  • Your checks are saved in History on your iPhone for 15 days. You can delete them at any time.
  • Counts of checks and audio minutes used are kept on your iPhone to apply your plan's limits.
  • The microphone is used only while you are listening, and only after you allow microphone access.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not see your name, email or payment details.

Accuracy

Ratings are produced with the help of AI from public web sources and can be wrong. Always check the sources shown with each claim.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
